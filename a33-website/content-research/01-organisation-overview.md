# Content Research: A33 — Organisation Overview

## Source content gathered

**From the organisation's own web presence:**
- A33 is located at 7 Orphan Street, Cape Town City Centre, 8001.
- The concept combines a café, an art gallery and a retail space across three floors of one building.
- The upper floors are operated as "gadget-free" zones, encouraging visitors to disconnect from phones and other devices while they are on those levels.
(Source: A33 homepage, a33ishome.com)

**From third-party local business listings:**
- Confirmed trading address and general opening pattern (weekday hours around 7:00 AM–5:00 PM).
- Independent description of the space as "coffee shop meets art gallery," noting the pairing of specialty coffee with curated African art, and a description of the upstairs floors as screen-free zones for reading and quiet time.
(Source: Corner business directory listing for A33)

## What this means for the website content

The homepage, About page and Menu & Gallery page content were built directly around these three confirmed facts:
1. Three-floor structure (coffee / gallery / gadget-free).
2. Location on Orphan Street, Cape Town CBD.
3. The core brand differentiator — a deliberately tech-free upper floor experience.

## Content still to be confirmed with the organisation directly (flagged as assumptions)

The following details were **not found in public sources** and have been written as realistic, illustrative placeholder content pending confirmation from A33 directly. These are clearly assumption-based and should be replaced with real information before the site goes live:
- Specific menu items and prices
- Exact opening hours for weekends
- Names and bios of team members
- Current/upcoming exhibiting artists
- Shop product range and pricing
- Direct phone number and email address

This mirrors real freelance/agency practice: publicly available information is used as a factual starting point, and clearly-flagged placeholder content fills any gaps until the client provides final copy.
