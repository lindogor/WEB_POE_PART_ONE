About A33

Coffee, art and a little quiet — all under one roof on Orphan Street.

Our History

A33 opened as a small, independently owned concept space in Cape Town's city centre, built around a straightforward idea: pair specialty coffee with serious, curated African art, and give people a genuine reason to put their phones away for an hour. What started as a single café floor grew into a three-level building, with the upper floors set aside as fully gadget-free zones.

Mission & Vision

Mission: To create a space where quality coffee, contemporary African art and calm, mindful social interaction can coexist under one roof.

Vision: To be Cape Town's go-to cultural destination for people who want to disconnect from their screens and reconnect with art, coffee and conversation — while giving local artists a serious platform to show their work.

Our Team
