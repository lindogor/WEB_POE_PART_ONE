## Project Overview
This project is the design and development of a five-page website for **A33**, a small, independently owned café, art gallery and retail space located at 7 Orphan Street, Cape Town City Centre. A33 combines specialty coffee with curated African art across three floors, with the upper floors operated as gadget-free zones.

The website was chosen and approved as the Part 1 project proposal (see `/docs/Proposal.docx` or the submitted Word/PDF proposal) and is now being developed through static HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with future phases adding backend/CMS functionality as the module progresses.

## Website Goals and Objectives
- Increase foot traffic by clearly communicating A33's location, hours and in-store experience.
- Generate online sales of art prints and branded merchandise (planned for a later phase).
- Showcase featured artists and rotating exhibitions.
- Support enquiries and bookings for private events hosted on the gallery floor.

**Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):**
- Online store conversion rate (future phase)
- Average time spent on the Menu & Gallery page
- Number of event/exhibition enquiries submitted
- Growth in referral traffic from social media

## Key Features and Functionality
- Responsive, mobile-first layout across all five pages
- Consistent global navigation with an active-page indicator
- Coffee menu listing (`services.html`)
- Gallery / current exhibition section with a shop preview (`services.html`)
- Enquiry form covering event bookings, exhibition enquiries and product orders (`enquiry.html`)
- Contact page with an embedded map and a contact form (`contact.html`)
- Front-end form validation with inline feedback (`js/script.js`)
